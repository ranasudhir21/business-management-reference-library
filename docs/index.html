Business Management Reference Library

A curated, DOI-verified library of 351 business and management research papers, book chapters and reviews for researchers writing research papers, literature reviews and systematic literature reviews (SLRs), finding research gaps, building conceptual frameworks and hypotheses, and preparing peer reviews.

The library covers every article in FIIB Business Review (SAGE) from 2024 onward, every chapter of the Emerald book series Review of Management Literature, and the publications of Sudhir Rana. Collections: Rana publications (149), Review of Management Literature (78), FIIB Business Review 2024 onward (136).

Use it in Claude, LaTeX or a reference manager

Download the Claude skill and add it in Claude under Settings, Capabilities, Skills; Claude will then search and cite the library during literature reviews and paper writing. The BibTeX, RIS and CSV files import into Overleaf, Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote and JabRef. The full release is archived on Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24084354.
